Four-camera SD preview — frame-rate testing

All four SD pictures were confirmed working together in Chrome. Each tile has its own 5 / 10 / 15 fps preview setting (initially 5 fps). A rate change while playing automatically restarts only that camera's preview; allow a moment for the previous stream to close. Other cameras remain on their selected rates.

Each camera heading now shows In (encoded NVR video frames per second fed to FFmpeg) and Out (JPEG images per second sent by the gateway). Values are measured over a rolling five-second interval and refresh every two seconds. These counters do not guarantee visually distinct pictures, actual browser rendering rate or end-to-end latency.

The 5/10/15 switches control only browser preview conversion, not the NVR's SD encoder, exposure, recording or motion detection. HD and audio remain pending; recording playback is unchanged.
